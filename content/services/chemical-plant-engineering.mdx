# Chemical Plant Engineering

We provide multi-discipline engineering for chemical and petrochemical facilities — from conceptual process design through detailed engineering, safety reviews and construction support.

## Process Engineering

- Conceptual and FEED studies
- PFD / P&ID development
- Heat & material balances
- Equipment sizing and specification (vessels, columns, heat exchangers, pumps, compressors)
- Process simulation (Aspen HYSYS, CHEMCAD)

## Piping & Mechanical

- Piping design and layout (AutoCAD Plant 3D)
- Pipe stress analysis (CAESAR II)
- PSV / flare / relief system design per API 520/521
- Materials selection and corrosion evaluation

## Safety & Compliance

- HAZOP, LOPA and SIL workshops facilitated
- Layout safety spacing (API 752/753)
- Compliance with OSHA PSM, EPA RMP, local codes
