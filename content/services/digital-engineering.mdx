# Digital Engineering & AI

We apply BIM, digital twin and AI workflows to compress design cycles, reduce construction rework and deliver facilities that are data-ready from day one of operations.

## Digital Engineering Services

- **BIM management** — Revit/AutoCAD Plant 3D authoring, Navisworks clash detection, federated model coordination across disciplines
- **Digital twin** — as-built model handover integrated with CMMS and IoT data streams (SightMachine, AWS IoT, Azure Digital Twins)
- **AI-assisted engineering** — proprietary AI agents for specification generation, code check, calculation review and RFI triage
- **4D/5D construction planning** — schedule-linked model, quantity takeoff automation, cost forecasting
- **Data standards** — COBie, ISO 19650, client-specific asset data schemas
- **Custom engineering tools** — web-based calculators and workflow automations integrated to your existing design stack

## Why Digital

Digital engineering is not a feature add — it is how industrial projects are delivered to predictable cost and schedule today. Our digital team is embedded from FEED through handover so the "as-built digital" matches the physical plant.
