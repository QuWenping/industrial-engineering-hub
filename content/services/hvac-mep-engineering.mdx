# HVAC & MEP Engineering

We design integrated MEP systems for industrial facilities — balancing production uptime, occupant comfort, energy cost and code compliance across HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire protection and process utilities.

## Systems We Design

- **HVAC** — process comfort, cleanroom (ISO-class), warehouse ventilation, lab fume hoods
- **Process cooling** — chilled water, cooling towers, process refrigeration, free cooling
- **Electrical** — medium-voltage service, switchgear, transformers, motor control centers, backup generators, UPS for critical loads
- **Plumbing & fire protection** — domestic water, process water, stormwater, sprinkler/hydrant systems, foam systems
- **Industrial utilities** — compressed air, nitrogen, vacuum, process exhaust
- **Lighting & controls** — high-bay LED, daylighting, BMS/SCADA integration

## Energy & Compliance

All projects are delivered with energy modeling aligned to ASHRAE 90.1 and local energy codes, with lifecycle cost analysis for high-efficiency options (heat recovery, VFDs, LED + daylighting controls).
