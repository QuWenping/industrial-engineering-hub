# Structural Engineering

Our structural engineering team designs safe, code-compliant and cost-optimized structures for industrial facilities — from superstructure framing to foundations, equipment supports and seismic retrofits.

## Engineering Capabilities

We work across steel, concrete, masonry and hybrid structural systems, delivering calculations, drawings and BIM models that are buildable, coordinated and ready for permit.

## Services

- **Structural analysis & design** — ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD, RAM modeling for gravity, seismic, wind and dynamic loads
- **Foundation design** — spread footings, piles, mat foundations, machine foundations with vibration control
- **Existing structure evaluation** — condition assessment, load rating, retrofit design
- **Industrial structures** — pipe racks, equipment platforms, crane runways, conveyor supports
- **BIM coordination** — Revit/Navisworks clash detection with MEP and process
