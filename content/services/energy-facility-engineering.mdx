# Energy Facility Engineering

We deliver multi-discipline engineering for energy infrastructure — from fossil generation balance-of-plant to renewable energy (solar, wind, battery storage) and substation/interconnection projects.

## Project Types

- **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)** — containerized and building-scale, fire protection, HV/MV interconnect
- **Solar PV & Wind** — site civil, MV collection, substation, transmission interconnection
- **Thermal generation** — boiler/turbine balance-of-plant, HRSG, cooling systems, emissions controls
- **Substations & transmission** — MV/HV switchgear, protection & control, relay coordination

## Multi-Discipline Delivery

Our civil/structural, electrical, I&C and mechanical teams coordinate to deliver constructible designs with grid code compliance (IEEE, IEC, local utility requirements) and black-start / safety studies.
