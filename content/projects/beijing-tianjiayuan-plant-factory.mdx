## Overview

China's first urban-pastoral complex, located in Tianjiayuan village, Dianjia town, Fangshan district, Beijing. Anchored in the Fangshan master plan's modern-agriculture strategy, it uses smart-agriculture technology as its core engine to develop water-saving, energy-efficient, environmentally-friendly agriculture and lift the regional agricultural value chain.

## Scope

- Agriculture + research + enterprise incubation + farmer skills training + farming-cultural creative + agri-tourism — a first/second/third-industry-integrated complex
- Introduces Panasonic's globally advanced vertical-farming technology, supported by leading domestic aerospace-food, herbal-medicine, and crop-seed research institutions

## Key technical highlights

- **Planning**: strict control of green rate, FAR, building density and height.
- **Architecture**: civil-defense, high-rise commercial building, multi-level underground garage, mechanical parking, slab-strip perimeter, and large-area roof greening.
- **Structure**: a mix of civil-defense, steel and concrete structural forms.
- **MEP**: complex building program spanning civil-defense, retail, mechanical parking and hotel functions.

Beijing Fangshan territorial-spatial plan and Chinese building / civil-defense codes.
