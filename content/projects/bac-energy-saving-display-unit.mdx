## Overview

Built for BAC's national distributor conference, this unit demonstrates the real-time performance and reliability of BAC's closed-circuit cooling tower under actual working conditions. It integrates HVAC, electrical control, civil structure and digital display into one showcase — a comprehensive demonstration of the firm's technical integration and project management capability.

## Key technical highlights

- **Independent circulation system**: uses an independent water system (electric boiler + cooler) with skid-mounted integrated design — the display is fully self-contained, stable and reliable, and does not interfere with existing on-site equipment.
- **Full-parameter real-time visualization**: automatic collection, cloud storage and large-screen real-time display of temperature, flow, pressure and energy consumption data.
- **Economic balance**: VFD (variable-frequency drive) control strategies balance display effectiveness with system operating cost.
- **Multi-discipline collaboration**: HVAC, electrical, control and civil-structure teams worked synchronously to ensure unified space layout, interface matching and delivery standards.
- **Delivery result**: zero delay, zero accident, first-time-right — delivered on schedule with high client satisfaction, and produced a reusable standardized management checklist and workflow.
