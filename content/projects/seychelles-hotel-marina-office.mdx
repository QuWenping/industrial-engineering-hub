## Overview

In 2019 the studio was commissioned to undertake a set of building designs in Seychelles — an archipelago nation in the Indian Ocean off East Africa (capital Victoria, ~455 km² land, ~98,900 population). The commission covered a hotel, a yacht marina and an office building, each adapted to the local historical, cultural and architectural context.

## Scope

- Hotel, yacht marina and office building designs in Seychelles
- Delivered design drawings after multiple rounds of joint discussion

## Key technical highlights

- **Local integration**: designs respond to Seychelles' local history, culture and architectural style rather than transplanting a foreign idiom.
- **Overseas delivery**: a complete overseas commission successfully delivered, demonstrating the studio's capacity to serve international clients beyond China.

## Standards & references

Adapted to Seychelles local building regulations and tropical-coastal context.
