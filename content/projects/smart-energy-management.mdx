## Overview

A smart energy-management system built on a digital platform and deeply integrated with smart manufacturing. It provides statistical analysis and optimization for cooling, heating, water, electricity and gas energy data, achieving comprehensive management across intelligent, remote, visual, information and data dimensions.

## Scope

- Full-chain digital energy comprehensive design and service: from energy planning and investment/construction, through operation management, to digital systems and energy capital services
- Integration of generation, charging, storage, efficiency and sales (发充储节售) energy-system optimization technologies

## Key technical highlights

- **Classified-channel smart management**: the system uses classified communication channels for secure energy data transmission.
- **Five-in-one optimization**: integrates generation (distributed PV), charging, storage, efficiency and grid-sales into one coordinated system.
- **Customized solutions**: "full-link, one-stop, customized" digital energy solutions covering energy planning, investment, construction and operation.
- **IoT-driven**: all energy consumption data collected via sensors and analyzed for optimization, achieving remote, visual and data-driven management.

## Standards & references

Chinese energy-management and smart-manufacturing standards.
