## Key service highlights

- **Investment estimation**: precise cost estimation at feasibility/concept stage to support go/no-go decisions.
- **Budget preparation**: detailed construction budgets aligned with design drawings and market rates.
- **Cost control**: ongoing cost tracking and risk management through design and construction to keep projects within budget.
- **Multi-sector**: cost consulting across education, logistics, industrial parks, institutional and residential projects.
