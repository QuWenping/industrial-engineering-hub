## Overview

An industrial-heritage renewal inside Dalian's Wisdom Valley industrial park, converting a former factory into a comprehensive cultural-tourism destination of commercial experience, food, entertainment and amusement. Positioned as a city amusement park and a Dalian tourist landmark.

## Scope

- Site 4,800 m², GFA ~10,000 m²
- Industrial-to-commercial functional conversion with structural safety, retaining an industrial architectural style

## Key technical highlights

- **Structure**: the existing industrial-heritage structure was reinforced and the original industrial architectural style was kept.
- **MEP**: the owner's high-power ice-energy demands were fully safeguarded, with each zone subdivided to meet commercial and performance requirements.
- **Experience design**: Beibei Avenue and Screaming Street host the mechanical giant-bear "Beibei" meta-universe IP, with flagship shows ("Beibei Big Show", "North Wind Goddess Phantom Show"), a trendy food street and amusement rides — merging park, food, retail and study-tour formats into a new "city park and future community" experience.

## Standards & references

Chinese building, structural-strengthening and commercial-fire codes.
