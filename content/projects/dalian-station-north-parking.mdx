## Overview

A multi-level automated parking facility north of Dalian railway station — the largest of its kind in Northeast China — with a highly complex plan that drove non-uniform member layout and stiffness discontinuity, especially where the automated parking equipment imposes complex loads.

## Scope

- GFA 5,883.1 m²; capacity 676 cars (182 on the flat-move layer + 494 in the tower garage)
- 1,670 tonnes of steel; 1,535 drawings; 42.5 m tall, 6 floors above + 2 below

## Key technical highlights

- **Innovative structural system**: a frame-center-brace system was adopted. Compared with a conventional frame, it uses less steel (controlling cost) and yields better calculated indices for safety and stability.
- **Optimization outcome**: the design saved approximately RMB 3.5 million and avoided an engineering accident that the original foundation-design defects would have caused.
