# Energy Facility Engineering

We deliver multi-discipline engineering for thermal generation, renewable energy (solar, wind) and battery energy storage facilities — from balance-of-plant design through grid interconnection and controls.

## Engineering Challenges

Energy projects combine MV/HV electrical work, strict grid compliance, protection coordination and multi-vendor equipment integration — with safety and reliability as the primary design constraints.

## Our Engineering Approach

Our electrical, civil/structural and I&C teams coordinate closely with utilities and equipment vendors to deliver designs that meet grid code requirements, pass utility review and commission on schedule.
